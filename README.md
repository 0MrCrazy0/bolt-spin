- **Levels, daily goals and over 500 achievements** to chase
- 23 music tracks

## 📱 Install it like an app
Open the game on your phone or computer, then:
- **iPhone/iPad (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android/Chrome:** tap the menu, then **Install app**

It works offline after your first visit.

## 💾 Back up your progress
Your progress is saved in your browser. Clearing your browser data wipes it,
so make a backup now and then:

Open **Options** and tap **⬇ Backup** to save a backup file you can keep.
To get your progress back, tap **⬆ Restore** and pick that file.
