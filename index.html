⚡ HOW TO PLAY

Line wins — Match 3+ symbols left to right on a payline. Higher symbols pay more. 50 paylines.

Spin & bet — Pick a machine, set BET and multiplier, press SPIN. Wins show on the WIN meter. TAKE banks a paused win without spinning again.

Hold & Spin — Land 6+ cash orbs anywhere. Orbs lock with 3 respins; each new orb resets to 3. Fill all 15 = GRAND. Banner shows locked count (e.g. 7/15). 4–5 orbs = near miss “ALMOST!”.

Free Games — Land 3+ scatters. Spins run automatically and stack on retriggers. Each machine has its own free-game style (subtitle on the banner). 2 scatters = near miss “SO CLOSE!”.

🚂 Machine Express — During Hold & Spin, land this machine’s Express train. After Hold & Spin the board opens — land fireballs, fill all 8 for a Fortune bonus.

🚂 Bolt Train (rare · all machines) — Free Hold & Spin. Collect 3 matching Mini / Major / Grand trains for that pot (or Mini if none match).

Reward Wheel — Earn wheel tokens on wins only. 25 tokens = 1 spin. Can pay cash, XP, wheel tokens, or ⚡ Bolt tokens.

⚡ Bolt Cabinet — Premium feature machine. Costs 1⚡ per spin (no cash bet). Stronger features & free-game boost. Earn ⚡ only from the Reward Wheel (not from normal wins). Open with the bolt button next to the wheel.

Level up — Unlock machines up to Level 100. Tap a locked machine to see its unlock level. Higher bets earn more XP.

Audio — Options: Sound Effects, Machine Music, Lobby Music, Vibration. In-game: quick mute buttons bottom-left (bell = SFX, music note = machine theme).

Tips — Finish Free Games / Hold & Spin / Express before returning to lobby. Reopening a machine shows your last reel. Tap coin rain or big-win overlay to skip.
