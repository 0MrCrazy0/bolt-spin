⚡ HOW TO PLAY

Line wins — Match 3+ symbols left to right on a payline. Higher symbols pay more. 50 paylines.

Spin & bet — Pick a machine, set BET and multiplier, press SPIN. Wins show on the WIN meter. TAKE banks a paused win without spinning again.

High bet = high risk — Bigger BET and multipliers make wins larger when they land, but credits can drain fast on a cold run. Manage your stake. If balance runs low, features get a slight nudge to help you keep playing (not a guaranteed win).

Hold & Spin — Land 6+ cash orbs and/or an EXPRESS train. New locks reset to 3 respins. Fill all 15 = Machine Grand (that cabinet’s progressive — hard to fill). A single gold MINI / MINOR / MAJOR tag on an orb pays that machine jackpot (not 3-of-a-kind). Fill all 15 = Machine Grand.

Free Games — 3+ scatters. Spins play automatically; extra scatters add more games. Retriggers stack.

Machine Express — Land 1 EXPRESS train (🚂) during any H&S (including free Bolt Train H&S). When H&S ends, that machine’s 8-cell board opens with themed collectibles. Fill all 8 for a Fortune bonus (extra cash on top of collected cells) + total celebration. Not free spins. Purple tier trains (MINI/MAJOR/GRAND) are different — collect 3 matching for the 🚂 pot; they do not open Express.

Matching trains (purple) — During H&S, purple MINI / MAJOR / GRAND trains can land. Collect 3 of the same for the global Mini / Major / Grand pot (separate from Machine Grand). GRAND trains are rare. Bolt Train free H&S with no 3-match → Mini consolation.

⚡ Bolt Strike — Random bolt events:

• Bolt Cabinet — stronger strikes that can complete 6+ orbs or 3 scatters

🚂 Bolt Train (rare) — Free Hold & Spin on the machine you’re playing. Same 3-match train rules + orb cash.

Bolt Raffle — Ticket button. Mini pot every 20 min. 1 ticket per buy (max 10 per pot). More tickets = better odds & bigger prize. Sales close 5 min before draw. When you win, a red badge appears on the ticket — open and TAP TO CLAIM.

WIN meter codes — Feature flashes use short tags: H&S Hold & Spin · F-S Free Games · L-W line boost · EXP Express · then the $ amount returns.

Lifestyle — First purchase of each item grants XP + wheel tokens (and sometimes ⚡ on big items). Re-equip is free.

Reward Wheel — Earn wheel tokens on wins only. 25 tokens = 1 spin. Can pay cash, XP, wheel tokens, or ⚡ Bolt tokens.

⚡ Bolt Cabinet — Premium feature machine. Costs 1 / 5 / 10⚡ per spin (no cash bet). Stronger features & free-game boost. Earn ⚡ from the Reward Wheel, Daily / Weekly / Monthly claims, and rare Bolt Train GRAND — not from normal line wins.

Level up — 20 themed machines unlock from Level 1 to 195 (Cosmic Crown). Levels never cap — ranks rise with level or machines unlocked (Rookie → Immortal). Tap a locked machine to see its unlock level. Higher bets earn more XP. Lifestyle purchases grant a little XP on first buy only.
