⚡ HOW TO PLAY

Line wins — Match 3+ symbols left to right on a payline. Higher symbols pay more.

Hold & Spin — Land 6+ orbs. Orbs lock, you get 3 respins. New orb resets to 3. Fill all 15 for GRAND.

Free Games — Land 3+ scatters (each machine’s feature picture). Spins play automatically. More scatters during Free Games stack extra spins.

🚂 Machine Express — Every machine has a named Express board. You do not need 3 trains. During Hold & Spin, land 1 special Express train (this machine’s symbol). When Hold & Spin ends, that board opens. Fill all 8 cells for Fortune.

🚂 Bolt Train (rare · all machines) — Global high-value event: free Hold & Spin (3 matching trains → Mini/Major/Grand), cash, tokens, XP, Mini/Minor, or a free ride on this machine’s Express.

Reward Wheel — Earn 1 token per paid spin. 25 tokens = 1 spin. Prize wheel then 1×–5× multiplier.
