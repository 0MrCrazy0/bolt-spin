⚡ HOW TO PLAY

Line wins — Match 3+ symbols left to right on a payline. Higher symbols pay more.

Spin & bet — Pick a machine, set BET and multiplier, press SPIN. Line wins pay on the WIN meter (left of spin controls). TAKE banks a paused win without spinning again.

Hold & Spin — Land 6+ cash orbs anywhere. Orbs lock with 3 respins; each new orb resets spins to 3. Fill all 15 cells for GRAND.

Free Games — Land 3+ scatters. Spins run automatically. Extra scatters during Free Games stack more spins.

🚂 Machine Express — During Hold & Spin, land this machine’s Express train. After Hold & Spin, that board opens. Fill all 8 cells for a Fortune bonus.

🚂 Bolt Train (rare · all machines) — Free Hold & Spin event. Collect 3 matching Mini / Major / Grand trains for that pot (or Mini if none match).

Reward Wheel — Earn tokens on wins only (more on bigger wins). 25 tokens = 1 spin. Prize wheel, then a 1×–5× multiplier. Use Auto spin on the wheel; Options can ask you to confirm every 5 / 10 / 20 auto spins.

Level up — Unlock machines up to Level 100. Higher bets earn more XP (more risk).

Tips — You cannot return to the lobby during Free Games, Hold & Spin, or Express Board — finish the feature first. Reopening a machine shows your last reel result. Tap coin rain / big-win overlay to skip. Sound & vibration are in Options.
