⚡ HOW TO PLAY

MACHINE RULES
Pick a machine above for full rules (free package, meters, Express, boosts).
Quick map
• MINI/MINOR/MAJOR board orbs → machine meter (1 pays)
• 3× purple trains → shared 🚂 pot
• EXPRESS (no tier tag) → 8-cell board after H&S
• ⚡ Bolt Pot → Bolt Train free H&S only

Line wins — Match 3–5 same symbols left→right on a payline (50 lines). Pays scale with your total stake (bet × multi). High 5-oak can pay near 1× stake; multi-line hits stack. Orbs / trains / scatters are separate (features).

Spin & bet — Pick a machine, set BET and multiplier, press SPIN (hold SPIN for auto). Wins show on the WIN meter. Bigger wins show a BIG WIN / MEGA WIN total — press SPIN again (button stays SPIN) to clear the pause and keep playing.

High bet = high risk — Higher BET / multi pays bigger when you hit, but a cold run can empty the bankroll quickly (like real machines). Manage stake size. At low balance the game gives a slight feature nudge so sessions can recover — not a guaranteed win, and not a safety net for max bets.

Hold & Spin — Land 6+ cash orbs and/or an EXPRESS train. New locks reset to 3 respins. Fill all 15 = Machine Grand (that cabinet’s progressive — hard to fill). A single MINI / MINOR / MAJOR board-color orb pays that machine progressive (1 orb — not 3-of-a-kind). Fill all 15 = Machine Grand.

Free Games — 3+ scatter symbols (feature pics). Base package is 10 or 12 free spins depending on the machine (+2 per extra scatter). Retriggers stack. See Machine Rules above for each cabinet.

Free + Hold together — If a spin lands 6+ orbs and 3+ scatters (or H&S during Free Games), you play Hold & Spin first (then Express if an EXPRESS train locked). When that finishes, Free Games resume with remaining spins. Same if Express follows Hold during a free session.

Scratch & Win — Random themed mini-game (base or during Free Games). Match 3 prize amounts under the covers to win — all three matched amounts pay. Each machine has its own theme (e.g. Pearl Plunder, Bolt Strike). On the secret cabinet it is Poop & Wipe. Does not spend a free spin when it triggers during F-S; Free Games resume after.

Machine Express — Land at least 1 EXPRESS train (🚂 with no Mini/Major/Grand tag) during H&S. When H&S ends, that machine’s 8-cell board opens. Extra EXPRESS symbols do not open a second board — they boost this board (2+ → extra respin, 3+ → seeded cells). Fill all 8 for a Fortune bonus. Regular machines: cash $ and rare MINI/MINOR/MAJOR. Bolt Premium only: also ⚡ money orbs, +1 ⚡ bolt-token orbs, and 🎡 +1 wheel spin. Not free spins.

Purple tier trains — During H&S, purple trains tagged MINI / MAJOR / GRAND can land. Collect 3 of the same tag for the shared 🚂 pot. They do not open Express. No 3-match = no train pot (locked orb cash still pays).

Board-color jackpot orbs — MINI / MINOR / MAJOR match the prize boards. Boards scale with your bet × multi (higher risk → bigger meters). 1 orb pays the board (not 3×), then the meter resets. Rare on the Express board too. 🚂 pots also scale with your stake.

⚡ Bolt Pot — Global pot during Bolt Train free H&S only: 3× GRAND trains, full 15 board, or a rare strong grid. Separate from machine progressives.

⚡ Bolt Strike — Random bolt events:
• Normal machines — rare bonus (cash, tokens, XP, +1⚡, or a symbol spark; can finish a near 5-orb / 2-scatter)
• Bolt Token Premium — stronger strikes that can complete 6+ orbs or 3 scatters
• After Hold / Express — bolts hit each won orb while the WIN total counts up (celebration)

⚡ Bolt Token Premium — token-premium machine. Stake 1⚡ / 5⚡ / 10⚡ per spin (higher ⚡ = higher $ stake & wins). Stronger features and Free Games. Earn ⚡ mainly from the Reward Wheel, raffle, and some rewards.

🚂 Bolt Train (rare) — Free Hold & Spin on the machine you’re playing. Same 3-match train rules + orb cash.

Bolt Raffle — Ticket button. Mini pot every 20 min. 1 ticket per buy (max 10 per pot). More tickets = better odds & bigger prize. Sales close 5 min before draw. When you win, a red badge appears on the ticket — open and TAP TO CLAIM.

WIN meter codes — Feature flashes use short tags: H&S Hold & Spin · F-S Free Games · L-W line boost · EXP Express · then the $ amount returns.

Lifestyle — First purchase of each item grants XP + wheel tokens (and sometimes ⚡ on big items). Re-equip is free. Big-ticket items are the main cash sink when bankroll grows — pure flex, no pay-to-win on the reels.

Reward Wheel — Earn wheel tokens on wins only. 25 tokens = 1 spin. Can pay cash, XP, wheel tokens, or ⚡ Bolt tokens.

⚡ Bolt Token Premium — token-premium machine. Costs 1 / 5 / 10⚡ per spin (no cash bet). Stronger features & free-game boost. Earn ⚡ from the Reward Wheel, Daily / Weekly / Monthly claims, and rare Bolt Train GRAND — not from normal line wins.

Level up — 20 themed machines unlock from Level 1 to 195 (Cosmic Crown). Levels never cap — ranks rise with level or machines unlocked (Rookie → Immortal). Tap a locked machine to see its unlock level. Higher bets earn more XP. Lifestyle purchases grant a little XP on first buy only.

🏆 Achievements — 319 badges in the lobby ACH panel. Progress tracks spins, features, level, machines, lifestyle, raffle, race & bankroll. Claim XP when complete (Claim All available). Free starter bolts do not count toward ⚡ badges.

Free Games boost — Each machine has its own F-S style (extra wilds, multipliers, denser highs). It only applies during Free Games and shows on the green FREE GAMES banner as FREE BOOST · ….

⚡ Bolt Boost — Random global hot window (~10–13 minutes real time). Slightly higher feature odds on every machine while the gold banner is up. Not the same as Free Games boost.

Haptics — Vibration works on Android browsers when Haptics is ON in Options. iPhone Safari does not support the vibration API (Apple limitation).

Audio — Options: Sound Effects, Machine Music, Lobby Music, Vibration. In-game: quick mute buttons bottom-left (bell = SFX, music note = machine theme).

Tips — Finish Free Games / Hold & Spin / Express before returning to lobby. Reopening a machine shows your last reel. Tap coin rain or big-win overlay to skip.
